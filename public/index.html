Help & Support (ADHD Executive Function Guide)

Evidence-based principles for the CanDo visual companion.

1. Visual Chunking & Zero Inertia

Telling a child to "clean your bedroom" causes task paralysis because the brain cannot sequence 20 distinct actions at once. CanDo breaks every room into exactly 5 visual micro-steps with Popo modeling each action.

2. Zero Timer Anxiety

Countdown clocks and ticking timers trigger fight-or-flight cortisol spikes in neurodivergent minds. CanDo uses cosmic distance orbits instead of countdowns: no ticking numbers, no panic.

3. Low Battery Mode & Sensory Overload

On days when the cadet is exhausted or overstimulated, toggle Low Battery Mode. CanDo automatically hides non-essential steps and preserves only the 1-2 core habits, awarding full pride!

4. Good Enough is Good Enough (Self-Check 4.2a)

Perfection is the enemy of consistency. If a child makes an honest attempt, approve the photo or ask for 1 gentle touch-up rather than penalizing effort.

Parent Guide: The 4 Core Principles

1.2a Visual Chunking

Breaking complex room chores into exactly 5 visual micro-steps prevents ADHD task paralysis and overwhelm. Cadets focus on only one bite-sized action at a time.

1.2b Asymmetric Gamification

Cadets experience an engaging cosmic space adventure with Popo as co-pilot, while Commanders manage a calm, structured, and distraction-free control center.

1.2c Zero Timer Anxiety

No countdown timers or ticking numbers that spike fight-or-flight cortisol. Cadets work at their natural pace with Low Battery Mode support on tough days.

1.2d Photo Proof Approvals

Review cadet photo proofs with 1-tap approval, or send gentle touch-up requests without penalizing effort. Consistency over perfection!
