 ADHD-Friendly 5-Step Chores by Room

Use one step per screen with a matching Popo GIF, a large Done button, a progress bar, and one star after each completed step.

 1. Bedroom — Reset Your Room

Finish goal: Your room is ready and safe to walk through.

1. Put your dirty clothes in the basket. (Popo carries clothes.)
2. Put your toys in the toy box. (Popo puts away a toy.)
3. Put your books on the shelf. (Popo stacks books.)
4. Pull up your blanket. (Popo makes the bed.)
5. Put your pillow on the bed. (Popo celebrates.)

 2. Bathroom — Finish Your Routine

Finish goal: The bathroom is ready for the next person.

1. Flush the toilet. (Popo presses the flush.)
2. Wash your hands with soap. (Popo washes with bubbles.)
3. Dry your hands with the towel. (Popo dries hands.)
4. Hang up your towel. (Popo hangs the towel.)
5. Turn off the light and close the door. (Popo switches off the light.)

 3. Kitchen — Clear Your Place

Finish goal: Your dishes and eating space are clear.

1. Take your plate to the counter. (Popo carries a plate.)
2. Take your cup to the counter. (Popo carries a cup.)
3. Put your food scraps in the bin. (Popo uses the bin.)
4. Wipe your place at the table. (Popo wipes the table.)
5. Push in your chair. (Popo pushes in a chair.)

Safety: Use only cool food and safe dishes approved by a parent.

 4. Dining Area — Help Reset the Table

Finish goal: The table is ready for the next meal.

1. Take your plate to the counter. (Popo carries a plate.)
2. Take your cup to the sink or counter. (Popo carries a cup.)
3. Put your napkin in the bin. (Popo throws away a napkin.)
4. Wipe one part of the table. (Popo wipes side to side.)
5. Push in your chair. (Popo pushes in the chair.)

 5. Living Room — Put Your Things Away

Finish goal: Your things are off the floor and sofa.

1. Find one thing that belongs to you. (Popo looks around.)
2. Pick it up. (Popo picks up an item.)
3. Take it to its place. (Popo walks with the item.)
4. Put it away. (Popo places it on a shelf or in a basket.)
5. Check the area. (Popo gives a thumbs-up.)

 6. Laundry Area — Put Your Clothes in the Basket

Finish goal: Your dirty clothes are in the laundry basket.

1. Find one dirty piece of clothing. (Popo looks and points.)
2. Pick it up. (Popo picks up a shirt.)
3. Walk to the laundry basket. (Popo carries the shirt.)
4. Put it inside. (Popo puts the shirt in the basket.)
5. Check your floor. (Popo looks around and celebrates.)

Safety: Do not touch detergent, washing machines, or dryers without permission.

 7. Entryway — Get Your Things Ready

Finish goal: Your things are ready by the door.

1. Put your shoes together. (Popo lines up shoes.)
2. Put your slippers on the shoe rack. (Popo places slippers away.)
3. Hang up your coat or jacket. (Popo hangs a jacket.)
4. Put your backpack in its place. (Popo puts away a backpack.)
5. Put your hat or cap away. (Popo places a hat on a hook.)

Safety: Do not open the door for strangers.

 8. Study Area — Reset Your Desk

Finish goal: Your desk is ready for homework.

1. Put your pencils in the pencil cup. (Popo stores pencils.)
2. Put your books in one stack. (Popo stacks books.)
3. Put your papers in your folder. (Popo files papers.)
4. Put rubbish in the bin. (Popo throws away paper.)
5. Put your notebook in the middle. (Popo places the notebook.)

 9. School Bag Area — Pack Your Bag

Finish goal: Your bag is ready for school.

1. Take out rubbish from your bag. (Popo empties a bag.)
2. Put your homework in your folder. (Popo files homework.)
3. Put your books in your bag. (Popo packs books.)
4. Put your pencil case in your bag. (Popo packs the pencil case.)
5. Put your bag in its place. (Popo places the bag by the door.)

 10. Shared Room or Play Area — Tidy the Space

Finish goal: The walking area is clear.

1. Find one toy or item. (Popo looks around.)
2. Pick it up. (Popo picks it up.)
3. Take it to the correct place. (Popo carries it.)
4. Put it away. (Popo places it in a box or basket.)
5. Look at the floor. (Popo checks and celebrates.)

 After Every Five Steps

Show:

 Is the room clean enough for now?

Buttons:

- Yes
- No
- Send a photo
- I need help

If the child taps No:

 That’s okay! Let’s do one more small round.

If the child sends a photo before finishing:

 Your picture was sent. Your parent can check your progress.

After parent approval:

 Your parent approved your chore! You earned all your stars!

For safety, parents should enable only the rooms and chores that are appropriate for the child’s age and home.
